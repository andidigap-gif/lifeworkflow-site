How Rosa turned her pain into wisdom

Rosa Lazić · Soul Reiki Master · Bodyworker
50 min11 chaptersNovember 2026
Watch on YouTube soon Listen on Spotify soon Follow @trust.theprocess111
About this episode

Rosa and I grew up in the same village in the Bavarian Alps. Years later we ran into each other again in Cape Town, as roommates in the same house. She was the first person outside my family to hold my book.

In this conversation she tells how she grew up as the stranger, why she stopped talking about what she felt, and how the hardest years of her life cracked her open. We talk about AfrikaBurn, about the night she danced the way she always wanted to, about what a Reiki master actually does, and about why a book can read differently every time you pick it up.

What you'll take away

Six things Rosa remembers

02:29 · Growing up

Home is a feeling, not a place

In Germany she was the foreigner. On holiday in Bosnia and Serbia she was the German. Home stopped being somewhere to find and became something to feel.

“I always thought that home is a place. Now I know it's different.”
09:32 · Pain into wisdom

The hard times crack you open

There is no sunshine without shadow. Rosa doesn't hide from the worst years anymore. She sees them as what made her who she is today.

“The harder the hit, the more it cracked me open, and the more my light is able to shine.”
11:41 · AfrikaBurn

Let yourself be different in public

At home she always danced differently. In public she danced like everyone else. In the desert she stopped switching herself off, and that was the turning point.

“I allowed myself to dance the way I always wanted to dance publicly.”
22:22 · Pockets of wonder

Feel it once, then find it again

A week in the desert doesn't solve your life. But once you know how full presence feels, you can open small pockets of it in your everyday life and stretch them out.

“It's easier to remember that state of mind in your daily life.”
23:46 · What Reiki does

Your body heals when it feels safe

Rosa explains Reiki without the mystery: the nervous system has a stress mode and a healing mode. Her work is to help you switch to the second one.

“Your body is only able to heal when you're not in the fight or flight mode.”
43:37 · Resonance

A book meets you where you are

Fifteen years ago The Power of Now was hieroglyphs to her. Today she reads it once a year and finds something new each time. She expects the same from Trust the Process.

“Each and every time I'm reading it, I resonate with something different in the book.”
Little girl, there's going to be some really, really tough times, but keep going. It's going to be worth it.
Rosa, to her younger self, at AfrikaBurn

The guest

Rosa Lazić

Soul Reiki Master and bodyworker. Born in Garmisch-Partenkirchen to Yugoslav parents, plant-based for more than thirteen years, living between Cape Town, Germany and the road. Right now she is building something new: a Reiki session that moves to music, her own flow state turned into healing.

“I don't consider myself as a guru. I consider myself as a person who just remembers of what we are capable of as human beings.”
