What you might be thinking

Fair questions

"I don't have time for this right now."

That's usually the clearest sign. If there's no room for one honest conversation, the autopilot is already running the show. We start with what gives you energy back fastest.

"I'm not into spiritual stuff."

Neither is this, at least not the vague kind. It's grounded in psychology (Jung, archetypes, the hero's journey) and in a very practical question: what does your week actually look like?

"Isn't this just coaching or therapy?"

Therapy works on what's broken and on the past. Most coaching sells hustle and optimization. Life Work Flow is for people who have "nothing wrong" and still feel something missing, and it looks forward.

"Why you?"

Because I lived it inside a real career, not instead of one. Remote since 2018, sales leader, years across three continents, and more than one reinvention. I know both worlds, and I'll tell you honestly if I'm not the right person for you.
